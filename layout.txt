ACOS math plugin is a mathematical plugin full of mathematical functions, constants, conditions, and more to make programming easier.



Functions:
	-All trigonometric functions (sin, cos, tan, csc, cot, …)
	-All hyperbolic trigonometric function (sinh, cosh, tanh, …)
	-Special functions like Gamma, Zeta, Error function, …
	-Operations (sqrt, exponentiation, …)

Constants: 
	-Pi (float & double)
	-e (float & double)
	-macheroni constant
	-goldenratio
	-gravitational power
